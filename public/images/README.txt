Put YOUR OWN images in these folders (nothing here is stock or fake):

  artworks/     -> your drawings & paintings   (listed in public/content/artworks.json)
  photography/  -> your photographs            (listed in public/content/photographs.json)
  sketches/     -> sketchbook pages            (listed in public/content/sketches.json)
  process/      -> process step images         (listed in public/content/process.json)
